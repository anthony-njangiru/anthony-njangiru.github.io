Systems he's built

A sample of the operational systems Anthony has designed and now runs day to day.

National scheduling & data-coordination system

Designed and runs the scheduling and data-coordination backbone for a programme spanning 21+ counties — a Smartsheet-based structure linking an IPTT and donor logframe together, with mobile data-collection forms and conditional alerts that flag scheduling conflicts before they become delays.

21+ counties, 4,000+ schools tracked, 25% faster on-time completion

Somaliland national cluster tracker

When the programme expanded into Somaliland, Anthony designed the country's first cluster-tracking system from scratch — following trainers of trainers and sub-TOTs through to teach-backs and community sessions — and trained ten local staff to run it.

22 clusters and 60+ teach-backs logged in year one

Weekly field-activity scheduling system

A shared weekly template that coordinates staff activities, venues and status across every region at once — running continuously for more than a year without a gap, and giving programme leadership one place to see what happened, what's cancelled, and what's next.

60+ consecutive weekly cycles across all regions

Safeguarding & GBV-prevention M&E dashboard

Turned raw, county-level survey submissions from a gender-based-violence-prevention curriculum into an automated county-performance dashboard — supporting safeguarding decisions and referral-pathway reporting for programme teams, without a manual pivot table in sight.

County-level reporting, built from raw field submissions
